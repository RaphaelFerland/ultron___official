Privacy Policy — Ultron Interactive

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Ultron Interactive ("we", "us", "our") operates the Instagram account @ultron___official, an automated character/assistant that replies to direct messages using AI-generated responses. This policy explains what information we collect when you interact with this account and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you send a direct message to @ultron___official, Instagram provides us with:

  • Your Instagram-assigned sender ID
  • Your Instagram username
  • The content and timestamp of the messages you send

We do not request or collect any information beyond what is necessary to receive and respond to your messages. We do not access your posts, story activity, follower list, or any data unrelated to direct messages sent to this account.

How We Use Your Information

Information you send is used to:

  • Generate an automated, AI-written reply to your message
  • Maintain conversational context across messages you send over time (e.g. remembering earlier parts of a conversation with you), so replies are consistent rather than starting from nothing each time

Third-Party Processing

The text of your messages is sent to Groq, Inc., a third-party AI inference provider, solely to generate a reply. Groq processes this text under its own privacy terms and is not authorized by us to use it for any purpose other than generating the response returned to us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage & Retention

Message content and the derived conversational context described above are stored locally by the account operator and are retained until you request deletion (see below) or until the operator removes them.

Your Rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, any information we hold about your Instagram account by contacting us at the email below. We will respond to and fulfill verified requests within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

This account is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
